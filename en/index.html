04 About

Who I am

I’m Luis Henrique, I’m 18 and I study Software Engineering at Fametro, in Manaus, Brazil. I started programming out of necessity: I wanted to solve problems that off-the-shelf apps didn’t solve the way I needed.

Since then I’ve built systems I use every day and tools for people close to me — such as a personal organization system with 19 modules and an anatomy study platform with a navigable 3D model.

I enjoy the part that rarely shows: modeling the database, separating business rules from input and output so they can be tested, and deciding what not to put in a project. I write architecture docs because they force me to justify my choices — and because whoever comes next should understand the system without having to ask me.

I learned the technologies I use because a project needed them, not because they were on the syllabus. That’s how I intend to keep going.
